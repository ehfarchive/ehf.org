This video was filmed at New Frontiers / Te Tūhura Nuku - November 2018 in Upper Hutt, New Zealand.

New Frontiers is hosted by the Edmund Hillary Fellowship (EHF), a global community of changemakers based out of Aotearoa New Zealand. EHF brings together humankind’s creative potential and entrepreneurial spirit in New Zealand, to build and support innovative ventures for global impact, in partnership with New Zealand's Global Impact Visa.
