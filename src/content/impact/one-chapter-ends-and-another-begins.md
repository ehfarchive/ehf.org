*Our team and the New Zealand Government set out to do immigration in a new way and launched the Global Impact Visa pilot. After 40 months since launching EHF with the Global Impact Visa, the Fellowship is now closed for new applications.*

### The pilot has been a massive success

As we complete the application phase, we are humbled and grateful to the thousands of people that have applied to join us in this work. Over the coming months the Fellowship will grow to 8 cohorts of impact focused entrepreneurs, investors and change-makers building solutions to pressing global challenges.

### The next leg of the journey: Activating impact in New Zealand

As humanity navigates through unprecedented times, we remain deeply committed and focused on the next phase of our journey. The next chapter for EHF is about activating the impact of the Fellowship in New Zealand. More on this to come soon. We will also be in touch in 2021 about the future of the Global Impact Visa. **

***–Thank you from the EHF Team***
